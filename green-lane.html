Green Lane Gutter Inspection Points

Wooded reservoir-area properties need regular attention. Use this checklist after every major storm season:

Branch Impact Damage

Overhanging tree branches deposit debris and physically dent gutters during wind storms. Check for dents that alter pitch or hold water.

Pine Needle Accumulation

Pine needles compact into a dense mat invisible from the ground but completely blocking water flow. Check in October before oak leaves add to the load.

Shingle Granule Buildup

As asphalt shingles age, granules wash into gutters and create a heavy sediment. This adds weight and reduces drainage capacity significantly.

Mold & Moss in Standing Water

Ponded water in flat gutter sections grows mold quickly in Green Lane's shaded, damp environment. Correcting pitch eliminates this entirely.
