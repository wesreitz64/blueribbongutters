Collegeville sits in a low-lying pocket of Montgomery County where rainfall drains into the Perkiomen Creek and its tributaries. When heavy storms roll through the 19426 zip code, homeowners near Providence Road and Route 29 face a challenge that flat-land suburbs don't: water has nowhere to run.

### The 3 Most Common Drainage Problems We See in Collegeville

**1. Downspouts that end at the foundation**
This is the single biggest mistake in older Collegeville homes. A downspout dumping 1,000 gallons of rainwater right against a block foundation will cause water infiltration every time. The fix is a proper underground drain extension or a surface-grade splash block and extension tube that directs water at least 8 feet from the house.

**2. Undersized 3-inch downspouts on large roof areas**
Many Collegeville colonials have long rear roof expanses that drain to a single downspout. One downspout can only handle so much volume before it overflows. We frequently upgrade these to 3x4 rectangular downspouts, which handle roughly 40% more water.

**3. Downspout elbows pointed the wrong direction**
The bottom elbow of a downspout needs to direct water *away* from the house at grade. We often find them pointing parallel to the foundation wall, creating a river along the base of the home.
