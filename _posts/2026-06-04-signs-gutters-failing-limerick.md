Gutters are easy to ignore. They sit up on the roofline, out of sight, doing their job quietly — until they stop doing it. By the time most Limerick Township homeowners notice a problem, water has already been damaging the fascia, soffit, or foundation for months.

Here are the signs I look for when doing inspections along Route 422 and throughout the 19468 zip code:

### 1. Paint Peeling on Fascia or Soffits
This is one of the earliest warning signs. When gutters overflow, water runs behind them and soaks the wood. Peeling paint means the wood is already absorbing moisture repeatedly — and rot isn't far behind.

### 2. Water Stains on Siding
A vertical streak of dark staining below a gutter section means water is consistently overflowing or leaking at a seam. In Limerick's heavy clay soil environment, that overflow heads straight toward your foundation.

### 3. Sagging or Pulling Away From the Fascia
Gutters sag when hanger screws pull out of rotted wood, or when the weight of trapped debris and water bends the aluminum over time. A sagging gutter doesn't drain — it ponds.

### 4. Basement Dampness After Rain
If your basement is wet or musty after every significant storm, look up before you look down. Gutter overflow or improper downspout discharge is the most common cause of basement water intrusion in Montgomery County homes.

### 5. Visible Cracks, Holes, or Rust
Once a seam opens up or a section cracks, the damage accelerates. Water always finds the path of least resistance.
