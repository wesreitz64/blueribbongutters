If you own a home in Limerick, Pennsylvania, you already know about the soil. Montgomery County is famous for its dense, heavy red clay. While it might be great for growing certain native plants, it is an absolute nightmare for your foundation.

When it rains heavily in the 19468 zip code, the water doesn't quickly drain away like it does in sandy soil. Instead, the clay acts like a sponge, absorbing water until it simply can't hold any more. 

### The Problem With Clay and Pooling Water

Once the clay is saturated, any excess rainwater pouring off your roof has nowhere to go. If your standard gutters are overflowing, or your downspouts are dumping water right next to your foundation walls, that water will sit directly against the concrete block or poured foundation of your home. 

This causes two major, incredibly expensive issues for Limerick homeowners:
1. **Hydrostatic Pressure:** The saturated clay expands and pushes against your basement walls with immense force. Over time, this pressure causes horizontal cracks and bowing walls.
2. **Seepage:** Constant water pressure will eventually find its way through even the tiniest hairline cracks, leading to a wet, moldy basement and ruined drywall.

### The Best Defense for Your Home

The absolute best way to protect your Limerick home is to ensure roof runoff **never** pools near the foundation in the first place.

* **Install Seamless Gutters:** Typical pieced-together gutters leak at the seams directly onto the foundation. Custom seamless aluminum gutters guarantee that the water safely travels all the way to the downspouts without spilling over.
* **Add Downspout Extensions:** Your downspouts shouldn't end right at the base of your wall. We install professional, heavy-duty downspout extensions to direct water at least 5 to 10 feet away from the foundation, safely distributing it onto areas where the soil can handle the load.

If you've noticed puddles near your house after a storm, don't wait for a flooded basement to take action.
