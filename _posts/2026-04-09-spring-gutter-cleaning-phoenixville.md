Spring arrives hard in Phoenixville, Pennsylvania. The warm spells along the Schuylkill River bring heavy rain, and the mature oak and maple trees lining colonial-era streets drop a season's worth of debris into gutters that have been frozen all winter.

### What Gets Left Behind After a PA Winter

After months of freeze-thaw cycles common in the 19460 zip code, gutters see more stress than at any other time of year. Here's what we check during every spring inspection in Phoenixville:

1. **Hanger separation:** Ice weight pulls hangers away from the fascia board. Gaps as small as a quarter-inch cause gutters to pitch incorrectly and pond water.
2. **End cap sealant failure:** Temperature swings crack the butyl sealant on end caps. You'll never notice until a heavy spring rain sends water pouring down the side of your house.
3. **Downspout blockages:** Winter debris compacts into the elbows. A blocked downspout can back up an entire gutter run in minutes.
4. **Fascia rot:** If gutters were pitching slightly toward the house all winter, the wood behind them may already be soft.
