When I do estimates in Douglassville, along Route 422 and through the Amity Township area, I frequently find homes still running the original 4-inch gutters installed when the house was built. These homes were often constructed decades ago when larger gutters were considered unnecessary or too expensive.

That math has changed.

### The Problem With 4-Inch Gutters

A standard 4-inch K-style gutter can handle about 1,000 square feet of roof area in a moderate rainstorm. Many colonial and split-level homes in the 19518 zip code have roof areas well above that threshold — especially when steep pitches are factored in. Steep roof pitches accelerate water runoff speed, which means your gutters need to capture it faster.

The result: overflow. Every time it rains hard, water dumps off the front of the gutter, straight down onto your foundation planting, walkways, and eventually your foundation.

### Why 6-Inch Gutters Solve This

Six-inch K-style gutters handle roughly 50% more volume than 4-inch gutters. For larger roof sections in Douglassville, they are simply the right tool. Paired with 3x4 downspouts instead of 2x3, the entire system moves water off the roof and away from the foundation at the rate Pennsylvania summer storms demand.

### The Seamless Advantage in Douglassville

Six-inch gutters show seams and imperfections more than 5-inch gutters do. Seamless fabrication on-site eliminates this entirely — the finished product looks factory-built because it is.
