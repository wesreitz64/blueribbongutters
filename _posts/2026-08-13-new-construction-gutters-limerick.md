Limerick Township has seen substantial new construction over the past decade, particularly in developments off Route 73 and Lewis Road. These are beautiful homes — but one thing I see consistently when I'm called out to do an estimate is builder-grade gutters that are already failing at 3 to 7 years old.

### Why Builder Gutters Underperform

When a production builder is finishing 40 homes in a development, the gutter subcontractor is bidding at the lowest possible margin. That means:

- **Thinner aluminum gauge:** Builder gutters are often installed in .019" to .025" aluminum. Blue Ribbon installs .032" — nearly 25% thicker. It means the difference between a gutter that dents when a ladder hits it and one that holds its shape for 20+ years.
- **Fewer hangers:** Industry standard is one hanger every 24 inches. Some builders stretch to 36 inches to save time and material. This allows the gutter to sag between supports under debris and ice weight.
- **Minimal pitch:** Gutters require at least ¼ inch of drop per 10 feet to drain properly. In a rushed installation, this gets eyeballed rather than measured.

### What We See in 19468 Developments

By years 5–8, many Limerick development homes show sagging gutter runs, seam failures at low points, and evidence of water intrusion at the fascia behind the gutters. At this point, the cheapest long-term fix is replacement with properly-spec'd seamless gutters installed at the correct pitch.
