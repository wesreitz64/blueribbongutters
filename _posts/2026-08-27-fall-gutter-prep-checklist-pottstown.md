August in Pottstown means summer is winding down — and the leaves aren't far behind. By October, the white oaks and silver maples lining the neighborhoods near King Street and around Ringing Rocks Park will be loading up gutters across the 19464 zip code.

Here's the checklist I walk homeowners through every fall:

### ✅ Pre-Fall Gutter Checklist

**1. Clean gutters of summer debris**
Bird nests, maple seeds, and storm debris accumulate all summer. Get these out before fall leaves add to the load.

**2. Check all hanger screws**
Grab a section of gutter near the center of each run and apply gentle upward pressure. If it flexes more than ¼ inch, a hanger may be loose or pulling from soft fascia wood.

**3. Inspect end caps and seams**
Run a garden hose from one end of each gutter run and watch for drips at the seams and end caps. Catching a failing sealant now — when it's warm and dry — is much easier than in November.

**4. Flush downspouts**
Drop the hose directly into each downspout opening. It should drain freely within 5–10 seconds. If water backs up, a blockage in the elbow or underground drain needs clearing before winter.

**5. Look for sagging**
Walk the perimeter of your home and sight down each gutter run. It should slope continuously toward its downspout with no flat or reverse-sloped sections.

**6. Consider guards if you cleaned twice last year**
If your gutters needed two full cleanings last fall and winter, micro-mesh guards will pay for themselves in 2–3 years of avoided cleaning costs.
