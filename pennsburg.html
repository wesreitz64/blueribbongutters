Pennsburg's 5 Gutter Warning Signs

Older homes in Pennsburg show these signs before a major water damage event occurs:

Paint Peeling Behind Gutters

A sure sign water is overflowing or leaking at a seam. The fascia wood is absorbing moisture and rot is forming.

Mud Splatter on Siding

When gutters overflow, rain hits the ground and splashes back up against the siding. Mud lines near the foundation are a giveaway.

Dark Staining on Soffit

Water wicking behind the gutter and through the soffit leaves dark organic staining. Look up at the underside of the roof overhang.

Clogged Downspout Elbows

Debris packs tightly into downspout elbows on Pennsburg homes with heavy oak coverage. Test each one with a garden hose every spring.
